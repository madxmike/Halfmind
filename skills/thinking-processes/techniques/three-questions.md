# Three Questions

## Use When

- The agent starts the analysis.
- The agent loses the thread of the design.

## Technique

Ask three questions in order:

1. What to change?
2. What to change to?
3. How to cause the change?

Answer question 1 before question 2. Answer question 2 before question 3.
Question 1 gives the problem and the root cause.
Question 2 gives the design and the validation.
Question 3 gives the obstacles and the plan.

The three questions give the agent a frame for the change.

## Failure Mode

The agent answers question 2 before question 1. The design solves the wrong problem.

Source: Goldratt, "What is this thing called Theory of Constraints", 1990.
