# Concrete Examples

## Use When

- An answer stays abstract, general, or opinion-based.
- The user states a want without a case.

## Technique

Ask for one real case from the past.
Ask for the steps, the data, and the result of that case.
Example question: "Walk me through the last time this happened."
Prefer a demonstration to an opinion.
A demonstration is stronger evidence than an acknowledgment.

Check:

- Does the case have a date, a person, or a number?
- Does the case change a section of the doc?

## Failure Mode

The doc carries a slogan and no case. The reader cannot test the design.

Sources:

- Clark and Schaefer, "Contributing to Discourse", 1989 (evidence of understanding).
- Fitzpatrick, "The Mom Test", 2013 (past facts).
